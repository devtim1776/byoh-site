Who we are

"BYOH Marketing Tool" is an internal tool operated by BuildYourOwnHeaven Team, the developer of the Roblox game Build Your Own Heaven ("we", "us"). Contact: buildyourownheavenroblox@gmail.com.

What the tool does

The tool uploads short videos about our own game to our own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube). It is used only by us, for our own accounts. It is not offered to other users.

Data we process

Access tokens for our own accounts (OAuth)The platform's login, which we complete ourselvesTo upload our own videos to our own accounts

We do not collect, store or process data of other users, viewers or followers through this tool. We do not sell or share data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on our own computer, in a local folder excluded from version control. They are never shared with third parties. Videos are stored locally before upload.

Retention and deletion

We keep tokens only as long as the connection is needed. Disconnecting the app in the platform's settings, or deleting the local token file, removes access immediately. Contact buildyourownheavenroblox@gmail.com for any request.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone other than us.

Changes

We update this page when the tool changes. The date above shows the latest version.
